Collaborators

“My collaborators are everything to me. Nothing would be possible without them, nor would I want to do it.” -Emily

Here’s what they say about her.

  • “Emily’s creativity knows no bounds. Dancing for her was a highlight of my career.”

    — Brandon Cournay

  • “Working with Emily over the course of several years has been integral to my growth as an artist in and out of the studio. Her collaborative process is playful and encouraging, and the work itself diverse, innovative and challenging. But what I've always admired most about Emily is her dedication to exercising fair, organized, and sustainable working conditions for dancers in an industry where it is largely lacking.”

    — Kacie Boblitt

  • "Emily is truly an exceptional choreographer that is committed to her collaborators through joy, transparency, and organization. Her work is a collaborative process, and naturally attracts people that are not only talented but are also fun to be around. As a dancer in Emily’s company, I was always inspired by her dedication and fully executed visions."

    — Britney Kerr

  • "I have often described Emily Schoen as an exotic animal, because she appears to be fearless. She is a voracious and skilled creator and when she sets her mind to something, it happens. Her continued steps into the void have landed her on solid footing as an artist with merit. I laugh and shake my head...fearless."

    —Nicole Wolcott

  • "Working with Emily has been a highlight of my dance career. Her balance between professionalism and play creates a space that feels productive and structured while allowing room for exploration, growth, and lots of joy. I felt my time and energy was valued and respected and the process was viewed as a collaboration which always made me feel supported as an artist. "

    — AJ Tasley Parr

  • “Emily is a very interesting and inspiring person and artist to discover. We met in Tunisia and very fast, I was impressed by her talent and her ability to make her projects happen and realize (a collaboration between Tunisia and the USA!) She knows how to deal with so many things at the same time, but she never loses her artistic feeling and singularity. She knows very well how to involve the dancers in her universe, while respecting each personality. For sure, working with Emily will stay a beautiful and valuable souvenir for me!”

    — Cyrinne Douss

  • "Emily is an intuitive choreographer with clear intent and a unique perspective. She has honed her own distinct movement vocabulary over the years and works to bring out the best in the other artists around her."

    — Kile Hotchkiss

  • "Emily's movement emulates her creative process; meticulous, informative, and stimulating."

    — Zack Betty

  • "Emily is a true privilege to work with. Not only is she a superb creator, but she is unequivocally kind, generous, and a joy to be around. I would jump at any chance to work with her again.”

    — Heather Conn
