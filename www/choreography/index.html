dro•mi•a

Choreography: Emily Schoen Branch in collaboration with Jane Cracovaner, marco farroni leonardo, Christopher Kaiser, Una Ludviksen
Music: Michael Wall (original composition), Ramleh, Mitochondrion, Aphex Twin
Lighting design: Trevor Cushman
Costume design: Jordan Fell
Dancers: Jane Cracovaner, marco farroni leonardo, Christopher Kaiser, Una Ludviksen
Understudy: Kate Hancock

“Enantiodromia”—a rare-use word of Ancient Greek origins—describes the phenomenon where an extreme state, like anger or euphoria, becomes fully realized and then immediately begins to turn into its opposite. Said another way, the fullness of something leads to its disappearance.

Commissioned by the University of Washington’s Chamber Dance Company.

Hollow Hearts

A dance film highlighting the acute effects of collective isolation. Mixing quietude and yearning, it portrays companionship through loneliness, and acknowledges all kinds of love–including the unreciprocated.

Director / Choreographer - Emily Schoen Branch
Dancers / Additional Choreography - Liane Aung and Karl Watson
Cinematographer - Jacob Rosen
Editor - Ashli Bickford
Colorist - Tristan Seniuk
Music - “Lived and Died Alone” by Shamir; Original song by Lindi Ortega

Run time: 5 minutes

 

Baryshnikov Arts Center’s — In The Studio with Emily Schoen

A look into Emily’s “BAC Space Residency” with her group, Schoen Movement Company, and guest artists from Tunisia, as they work towards an evening-length premiere that culminates their two-year partnership.

Baryshnikov Arts Center named Emily the Martha Duffy Resident Artist for this residency, an award created by BAC Board Member James H.Duffy in memory of his late wife Martha Duffy, a former senior editor in charge of coverage of the performing arts for Time magazine.



 

See me in your eyes — An American and Tunisian Collaboration

An evening-length work culminating a two-year partnership between American and Tunisian dancers, featuring stories recounted from the dancers’ childhoods as they grew up across the world from each other. This work was supported by Dance Motion USA / Brooklyn Academy of Music, US Embassy in Tunis, American Dance Abroad, Queens Council on the Arts, and LaGuardia Performing Arts Center.

Cast: Houcem Bouakroucha, Alexandra Doyle, AJ Parr, Kais Harbaoui, Mohamed Ali Cherif, Emily Schoen Branch, Cyrinne Douss, and Monam Khemis

Run time: 55 minutes
