  • Emily Schoen Branch

    DIRECTOR / DANCER

    Emily Schoen Branch is a Dance Magazine “Top 25 to Watch” artist. She was Baryshnikov Arts Center’s Martha Duffy Resident Artist in 2019 (New York), a recipient of the Gibney Dance boo-koo grant for emerging artist in NYC, and nominated by METdance (Texas) for a Princess Grace Fellowship.

    Emily has fulfilled 19 choreographic commissions for companies and university programs across the country, including Olympic Ballet Theater (Washington), Santa Barbara Dance Theater (California), and Columbia Ballet Collaborative (New York).

    Her group, Schoen Movement Company, was home to her creative endeavors in New York for 6 years. They were presented by the historic Judson Memorial Church, WestFest, Jamaica Performing Arts Center, Liberty Hall Museum, and Joe’s Pub / DanceNOW. They toured their original happy-hour performance series “A Movement Flight” to microbreweries in 5 states, and maintained a long term collaboration with Tunisia-based dancers which included an international residency and livestream performance at the Tunisian National Theater and an evening-length premiere in New York. Baryshnikov Arts Center, Dance Motion USA / Brooklyn Academy of Music, Queens Council for the Arts, LaGuardia Performing Arts Center, and the US Embassy in Tunis supported Emily’s efforts.

    As a dancer, Emily worked for Kyle Abraham/AIM; the Metropolitan Opera in works by Doug Varone, Mark Morris and Carolyn Choa; the Rolling Stones in their 50th Anniversary Reunion Tour, and for 8 years with Larry Keigwin / KEIGWIN + COMPANY. With Larry Keigwin, she additionally served as collaborator, assistant, and repetiteur.

    Emily earned her BFA summa cum laude from the University of Arizona.

  • Ashley Menestrina

    DANCER

    Ashley Menestrina is a movement artist, performer, teacher, and choreographer. Her repertoire of solo works: Always a Creature , The Human Condition: Absent Presence, and Combative Echoes have been performed in eight countries and received numerous awards from festival juries. After moving to Seattle, Ashley created a new work thanks to Velocity Dance Center’s Bridge Project program. This creation was performed at SIDF and Gonzaga University where she also commissioned a new work for their repertory company. Additionally, Ashley worked with Anna Mlasowsky for the Amazon A.I.R program and performed a collaborative piece at the Amazon Spheres.

  • Audrey Rachelle

    DANCER

    Known best for her performances as Lady MacBeth in Sleep No More, Audrey has performed for 13 years with prestigious companies including Punchdrunk International, Hubbard Street Dance Chicago, Nashville Ballet, and The Equus Projects. Her experience extends on-camera with appearances in Comedy Central’s Broad City, Vogue Taiwan, and featured roles in the award-winning films Asphalt, Muscle, & Bone, Seasoned, T H R E E, and F O U R. As co-director of AnA Collaborations she has produced over 25 multidisciplinary works and has received support from 4Culture, Shoreline Public Art, The Warhol Foundation, Northwest Film Forum among others.

  • Una Ludviksen

    DANCER

    Una Ludviksen is a freelance dance artist and nonprofit arts administrator. She was born and raised in Seattle and holds a BFA from SUNY Purchase Conservatory of Dance. She also studied abroad at the Amsterdam University of the Arts, training with Marta Coronado and Min Li. Una has performed for the Merce Cunningham Trust in LA, Toronto, and New York, and dances for GREYZONE NYC and Crossman Dans(c)e. After moving back to Seattle last year, she performed with Natalie Fernandi and Tess Neill for Show 2, produced by co— (Emma Lawes + Maya Tacon).

  • Tariq Mitri

    DANCER

    Tariq Mitri is a freelance dance artist based in Seattle, WA. Originally from Abu Dhabi, U.A.E and raised in Portland, OR, he graduated from California Institute of the Arts in 2015. Since then, he’s worked in Los Angeles, Seattle, and New York and danced with MADBOOTS Dance, Kate Wallich + The YC, and WHYTEBERG among many others. From 2019 to 2021 he was the Rehearsal Director and Co-Director of Brooklyn based company HEIDCO. Tariq has had work presented at the Seattle Art Museum, the Seattle Erotic Arts Festival, and will be presenting a solo at Four organized by Co– (Maya Tacon and Emma Lawes).

  • Carol Davis

    DANCER

    Originally from Olympia, WA, Carol Davis moved to Seattle after graduating with her BFA in Modern Dance from Texas Christian University. While at TCU, Carol performed works by Adam Barruch, Elijah Gibson, Francisco Aviña and George Balanchine, among others. Since moving to Seattle in 2021, she has enjoyed getting to know the local dance community by way of teaching, choreographing, performing and her favorite: attending classes and workshops. Carol teaches full-time at Atlas Pilates and looks forward to dancing on your table.

  • Obadiah Freeman

    MC

    Obadiah Freeman is an actor, educator, and filmmaker. He has worked in multiple capacities for MTV, VH1, NBC Universal, FOX, CBS, and ABC. He currently provides talent for Commercial, TV, and Film production in the Pacific Northwest with representation from Topo Swope Talent. You can catch Obadiah on stage at the Pike Place Market- He's an active Ensemble Member at Unexpected Productions Improv; Seattle’s longest running improv theater!
